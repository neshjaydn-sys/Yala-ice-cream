Good to know

Questions, answered.

Where is YOLO Ice Cream?

You'll find us on Aga Khan Walk in Nairobi, Kenya. Tap Open directions to navigate straight to the shop.

How much does the ice cream cost?

Listed prices range from KES 530 for other favourites to KES 1,490 for the premium creations. Our listed prices come from an earlier menu brief, so please confirm current prices with the shop before visiting.

What are your opening hours?

We haven't published verified opening hours on this site. Please call +254 741 755 073 or check the YOLO Ice Cream Instagram stories before visiting.

Can I celebrate a birthday or group occasion at YOLO?

You're very welcome to celebrate with us. Call +254 741 755 073 or send a DM to @yolo_icec on Instagram and we'll help with the details.

Are the photos on this site the exact products?

No. Menu photographs are representative stock imagery matched to each creation's mood, and every card is labelled "Representative image". The real thing is even better.

How can I contact YOLO Ice Cream?

Call +254 741 755 073, or message @yolo_icec on Instagram. You can also find us on Aga Khan Walk in Nairobi.
